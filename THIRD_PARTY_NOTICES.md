## Algorithms from publications (own implementations)
- Fermat spiral fill: Zhao et al., "Connected Fermat Spirals for Layered Fabrication", ACM SIGGRAPH 2016
- Euler transformation fill: Gupta et al., 2021
- Contour-zigzag hybrid fill: Bi et al., 2022
- Arc fitting: idea after ArcWelder (FormerLurker, https://github.com/FormerLurker/ArcWelderLib)
- Curve bridging and self-intersection removal: idea after Laurent Delrieu's Nautilus plug-in
- Continuous-toolpath connection rule: Wesseler, Herrlein, Kiendl

## Process values from manufacturers
Guide values for nozzle, bead width and layer height, material temperatures and angled printing were derived from information supplied with the machines or published by the manufacturers: Weber Additive (Hans Weber Maschinenfabrik GmbH), DXR start package and slicer profiles; CEAD B.V., sheets "Angled printing (45 deg) design process" and "Nozzle size and layer width"; Ginger Additive. The values are our own evaluation, not manufacturer specifications, and have not been reviewed or approved by the manufacturers.
